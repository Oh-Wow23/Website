The Legend

From Olympus to your screen

The ancient Greeks prayed to Athena before every great undertaking. She guided Odysseus home, taught humanity to weave and build, and gave Athens the olive tree, a gift of lasting value over passing glory.

AthIna carries that spirit forward. Her name pairs Athena with AI, and her purpose is the same as the goddess's: to help people think clearly, choose wisely and make things well.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is AthIna?

AthIna is an AI assistant inspired by Athena, the Greek goddess of wisdom, strategy and craft. She answers questions, helps you plan, and works with you on writing, code and research.

Is AthIna always right?

No AI is. AthIna is designed to be careful: she explains her reasoning, cites sources where she can and says so when she is uncertain. For medical, legal, financial or other important decisions, double-check with a qualified professional.

What can I ask her?

Almost anything: history, science, maths, writing, coding, planning, language learning and more. If a question is outside what she can answer well, she will tell you.
